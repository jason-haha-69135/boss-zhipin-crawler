# stage2-query-generator Specification

## Purpose
Stage 2 (`core/query_generator.py`, endpoint `POST /api/resume/generate-queries`) is physically decoupled from Stage 1 and generates 10 multi-strategy natural-language job search queries (`recommended_queries`) from a sanitized Stage 1 candidate profile (`profile`) using `gemini-3.8-flash` (`temperature=0.4`, `query_count=10`) as the default generation model and `gemini-4-argon` (`temperature=0.0`) as the default LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation model in `eval/stage2_eval/`.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Decoupled Profile-to-Query Service Contract
The `core/query_generator.py` module SHALL operate as an independent Stage 2 service (`generate_queries_from_profile_with_gemini`) that accepts an in-memory Stage 1 `profile` JSON (carrying the user-selected BOSS直聘 `target_city` and `target_city_code`) without re-uploading or re-parsing the original resume file, and MUST include `target_city` and `target_city_code` in its JSON output.

#### Scenario: Generate 10 queries carrying BOSS直聘 target city from Stage 1 JSON
- **WHEN** `generate_queries_from_profile_with_gemini` receives a Stage 1 `profile` JSON with `target_city="武汉"` and `target_city_code="101200100"`
- **THEN** the module SHALL invoke `gemini-3.8-flash` (`DEFAULT_STAGE2_QUERY_MODEL`) at `temperature=0.4` (`DEFAULT_STAGE2_TEMPERATURE`) to return `query_count=10` (`DEFAULT_QUERY_COUNT`) `RecommendedQueryItem` objects
- **AND** the top-level JSON output SHALL include `"target_city": "武汉"`, `"target_city_code": "101200100"`, and `"target_cities": ["武汉"]`
- **AND** every item in `recommended_queries` SHALL include `"target_city": "武汉"` and `"target_city_code": "101200100"`

#### Scenario: Dynamically switch target city in UI and re-dispatch updated JSON to Stage 2
- **WHEN** a user dynamically changes the selected BOSS直聘 target city in the UI after Stage 1 parsing has already completed (for example switching from `"武汉"` to `"上海"`)
- **THEN** the UI SHALL update `currentResumeProfile` JSON with `"target_city": "上海"`, `"target_city_code": "101020100"`, and `"target_cities": ["上海"]`
- **AND** the UI SHALL automatically re-invoke `POST /api/resume/generate-queries` with the updated JSON without requiring the user to re-upload the resume
- **AND** Stage 2 SHALL return 10 newly generated queries whose JSON output reflects `"target_city": "上海"` and `"target_city_code": "101020100"`

### Requirement: Structured BOSS直聘 City Field Separation from Query Text
The `core/query_generator.py` module SHALL output `target_city` and `target_city_code` as independent structured JSON fields on each `RecommendedQueryItem` while keeping `query_text` completely free of city names and welfare terms.

#### Scenario: Keep query text free of city names while preserving structured city code
- **WHEN** Stage 2 generates queries for a candidate whose selected BOSS直聘 city is `"北京"` (`"101010100"`)
- **THEN** each `RecommendedQueryItem` SHALL contain `"target_city": "北京"` and `"target_city_code": "101010100"`
- **AND** `RecommendedQueryItem.query_text` SHALL NOT contain `"北京"` or any other city name
- **AND** if the LLM omits or hallucinates `target_city`, `_sanitize_queries_list` SHALL deterministically bind `target_city` and `target_city_code` to the validated BOSS直聘 city from `effective_profile`

### Requirement: Three-Element Query Syntax Without City or Welfare Terms
Every `query_text` generated by `core/query_generator.py` MUST follow the three-element natural-language syntax `"[目标行业/细分赛道]的[单一标准核心岗位名]岗位，月薪至少[X.Xw]"` and SHALL NOT contain any city names or work-hour/welfare terms.

#### Scenario: Enforce three-element structure with target industry role and salary floor
- **WHEN** Stage 2 generates or post-processes a `RecommendedQueryItem`
- **THEN** `query_text` SHALL explicitly contain the target industry domain, a single standard BOSS直聘 role title, and a minimum monthly salary floor in `"X.Xw"` format (such as `"互联网新媒体行业的新媒体运营专员岗位，月薪至少0.7w"`)

#### Scenario: Strip city names and welfare terms from query text
- **WHEN** the LLM output or user input contains city prefixes (such as `"深圳"` or `"武汉"`) or work-hour/welfare clauses (such as `"周末双休"`, `"五险一金"`, or `"年终奖"`)
- **THEN** `_strip_city_and_welfare_from_query` SHALL deterministically strip all city names and welfare phrases from `query_text` and ensure the three-element syntax remains intact

#### Scenario: Prohibit slash-concatenated compound role titles
- **WHEN** constructing the job role segment of `query_text`
- **THEN** the module SHALL output a single clean role title (2 to 10 characters) and MUST NOT concatenate multiple titles with `/` or `、` separators

### Requirement: Ten-Dimension Strategy Matrix and Salary Gradient
The `core/query_generator.py` module SHALL organize the 10 generated queries across the 10 orthogonal `STRATEGY_DIMENSIONS` (`strategy_tag`) with a progressive salary floor gradient anchored to the candidate's base salary floor $B$.

#### Scenario: Cover all 10 strategy tags with differentiated salary multipliers
- **WHEN** `query_count == 10` queries are generated for a candidate with base salary floor $B$
- **THEN** the 10 items SHALL map to the 10 defined `STRATEGY_DIMENSIONS` in order:
  - `#1 精准对口` ($1.00 \times B$): primary matching role in the core industry
  - `#2 晋升冲刺` ($1.15 \sim 1.30 \times B$): next-level supervisory or managerial role
  - `#3 核心行业优选` ($1.00 \sim 1.10 \times B$): secondary matching role within the primary industry
  - `#4 跨界拓展` ($1.00 \times B$): transferable-skill role in an adjacent high-growth industry
  - `#5 高薪突破` ($1.25 \sim 1.40 \times B$): high-paying sub-sector or high-value key account/technical role
  - `#6 细分垂直深耕` ($1.00 \sim 1.10 \times B$): specialized vertical domain role
  - `#7 头部名企/大厂` ($1.15 \sim 1.25 \times B$): standardized role in listed or large enterprise groups
  - `#8 复合技能溢价` ($1.10 \sim 1.20 \times B$): cross-functional role leveraging compound skills
  - `#9 高成长潜力岗` ($1.00 \sim 1.10 \times B$): core business role in emerging sectors
  - `#10 稳健保底优选` ($0.85 \sim 0.90 \times B$): high-interview-density safety role with a moderately relaxed salary floor

### Requirement: Python Role Anchor Bridging and Deterministic Fallback
The `core/query_generator.py` module SHALL bridge candidate profiles to high-frequency BOSS直聘 role titles via `_infer_boss_role_anchors` and `BOSS_ROLE_SYNONYMS`, and MUST provide a deterministic fallback generator when the LLM API is unreachable.

#### Scenario: Inject high-frequency BOSS role anchors into Stage 2 prompt
- **WHEN** preparing the Stage 2 prompt for `gemini-3.8-flash`
- **THEN** `_infer_boss_role_anchors` SHALL match `candidate_title`, `education_summary`, and `core_skills` against `BOSS_ROLE_SYNONYMS` to inject 6 to 10 high-recall role titles into the prompt

#### Scenario: Fallback to deterministic 10-strategy generator on network or API failure
- **WHEN** `create_interaction_with_fallback` raises an exception or returns fewer than 3 valid items
- **THEN** `build_queries_from_profile_fallback` SHALL deterministically construct 10 three-element queries covering all 10 `STRATEGY_DIMENSIONS` without failing the request

### Requirement: Stage 2 Automated Benchmark and LLM-as-a-Judge Gate
The Stage 2 evaluation suite (`eval/stage2_eval/evaluate_stage2_queries.py` and `eval/stage2_eval/llm_judge_stage2.py`) SHALL evaluate all 90 generated queries (9 qualified profiles $\times$ 10 queries) from `eval/dataset/stage2/stage2_qualified_manifest.json` using deterministic three-element and BOSS直聘 city-mapping checks plus `gemini-4-argon` (`DEFAULT_STAGE2_JUDGE_MODEL`) at `temperature=0.0`.

#### Scenario: Verify 100 percent syntax purity BOSS city code mapping and gemini-4-argon quality score
- **WHEN** `evaluate_stage2_queries.py` runs across `QUAL-01` through `QUAL-09`
- **THEN** `decompose_query_three_elements` and `evaluate_single_query_item` SHALL verify `three_elements_pass_rate == 1.0`, `no_city_or_welfare_pass_rate == 1.0`, `single_role_pass_rate == 1.0`, `target_city_pass_rate == 1.0`, and `pii_safety_pass_rate == 1.0`
- **AND** `evaluate_queries_with_llm_judge` using `gemini-4-argon` SHALL score each query on strategy alignment and resume grounding, archiving the full report to `eval/runs/latest_stage2_eval_report.json` with a mean score $\ge 9.0 / 10.0$
